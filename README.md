# Bronze Era

Bronze Era is a total conversion mod for **Europa Universalis V**, covering the period from **1209 BC to 120 AD**. Our main goal is to create a fun and immersive experience across the Bronze Age, Iron Age, Classical era, and beyond.

## Contributing

Thank you to everyone who contributes to the mod!

Anyone can help by submitting a **pull request**. The development staff will review each contribution and decide whether it should be accepted.

Regular and active contributors may also be invited to officially join the development team.

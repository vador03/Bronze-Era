# Brigantes Confederation

The Bronze Era start creates `brigantes_confederation` with `BRIGA` as its
leader and `LOPOC` and `METAN` as members. The organization uses a dedicated
type and the localized name "Brigantes Confederation" / "Confédération des
Brigantes". Its ten-year leadership score gives BRIGA a strong founding
advantage, while allowing another surviving member to lead if BRIGA vanishes.

Membership prevents members from declaring war on each other. When an outside
country attacks a member, the other members automatically join its defense.
The organization does not call members into offensive wars.

Members share food access and military access, gain 4% trade-center power,
3% selling efficiency, 0.25 diplomatic reputation and a small monthly tribal
cohesion bonus. The leader receives additional diplomatic reputation and
prestige. Members may leave and the three original tags may invite a former
member back; outsiders cannot join. The confederation dissolves below two
members.

The setup entry is in `main_menu/setup/1/15_international_organizations.txt`.
The rules are in
`in_game/common/international_organizations/01_bronze_brigantes_confederation.txt`.
An in-game start and defensive-war test remain useful to confirm engine
behavior.
